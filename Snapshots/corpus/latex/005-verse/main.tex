\section*{Notes beside the gate}

\begin{quote}At the low gate, count the hinges before measuring the leaf.\end{quote}
The ledger gives a second instruction.
\begin{quotation}Measure at the upper pin. A reading taken at the foot includes the slope of the path.\end{quotation}
A four-line mnemonic appears inside the cover.
\begin{verse}One mark at the hinge,\\one mark at the sill,\\a thread across water,\\a wheel standing still.\end{verse}
