\section*{Counting triangular stacks}

A stack gains one tile per row. Subtract two consecutive squares and sum:
\begin{multline}
 (2^2-1^2)+(3^2-2^2)+\dots+((n+1)^2-n^2)\\
 = (n+1)^2-1.
\end{multline}
\begin{align*}
 2\sum_{k=1}^n k+n &= (n+1)^2-1\\
 &= n^2+2n,\\
 \sum_{k=1}^n k &= \tfrac12 n(n+1).
\end{align*}
For four rows the tray therefore holds ten tiles.
