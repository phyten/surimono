\section*{A gate with a stop}

Let $u$ be the handle travel. The gate height is
\[
 h(u)=\begin{cases}0&u<0,\\u&0\le u\le 3,\\3&u>3.\end{cases}
\]
At the two stops we obtain
\begin{gather}h(-1)=h(0),\\h(3)=h(4).\end{gather}
\begin{gather*}h(2)-h(1)=1.\end{gather*}
