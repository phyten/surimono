\section*{Two reservoir readings}

\section{Averaging}For the pair $S=(2,6)$ the mean level and spread are
\begin{align}
 \mean(S)&=(2+6)/2=4,\label{eq:mean}\\
 d(S)&=\max(S)-\min(S)=4.\tag{R}
\end{align}
Equation~\eqref{eq:mean} places the mark halfway between the gauges.
