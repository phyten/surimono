\section*{Rack card format}

Separate the fields with \verb!|!. The literal \verb|#| introduces a comment.
\begin{verbatim}
# rack | shelf | slots
east   | upper | 04
east   | lower | 06
\end{verbatim}
Leading zeros keep paper labels aligned.
