\section*{Turning the sample grid}

One quarter-turn maps the east vector to the north vector:
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\begin{bmatrix}1\\0\end{bmatrix}
 =\begin{bmatrix}0\\1\end{bmatrix}.
\]
The worksheet prints the determinant, a bare grid and two delimiter samples:
\[
 \begin{vmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{vmatrix}=1,\quad
 \begin{matrix}a&b\\c&d\end{matrix}\quad
 \begin{Bmatrix}a\\b\end{Bmatrix}\quad
 \begin{Vmatrix}a\\b\end{Vmatrix}.
\]
