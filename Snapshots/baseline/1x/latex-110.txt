A spacing rule for folded stops
Fold Desk and Slot Desk
Abstract. Two card stops leave a measurable gap in a model rail. A fixed fold
preserves this gap when the stops move together.
1. A trial gauge
1.1. The common fold. Cut two stops on the same grid as the strip in Fig-
ure 1.
Figure 1. Rail for the folded stops

Theorem 1. Sliding both stops by one grid step preserves their separation.
Proof. Let the original marks be a and b. After a step of length t the gap is
(b + t)− (a + t) = b− a.
