A survey of the lantern court
Notes from the winter trial
Survey desk* Model workshop
Abstract
Three paper screens were placed along a model courtyard. We
recorded the first position at which the rear lantern disappeared.
1 Arrangement
A narrow screen stood beside each gate. The observer moved one tile at a
time.1
2 Result
The middle gate concealed the lantern first. Its screen crossed the sight line at
tile four.
