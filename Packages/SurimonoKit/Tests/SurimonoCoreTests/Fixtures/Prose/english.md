# Folding a sheet

This sample was written to try the writing check. The steps are made up and describe no real person or product.

Clear the desk and put down a single sheet. Check the the direction of the grain, then bring the near edge to the far edge.

Press the crease once with a ruler. A shallow crease opens again, and a crease that opens moves the next fold out of place.

She had had enough practice by then, and I think that that is why the last fold came out straight.

After folding the sheet in half, fold the left and right edges to the centre line as well, open the sheet again to see that four panels have appeared, compare the width of each panel with the others, and start over from the first fold whenever one of them is visibly wider than the rest of them.

Use `crease crease crease` only in code.
